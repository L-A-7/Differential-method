Source: Table 3.1 (chap-1-3.tex, \label{tab:md2D_gsolver_conique}), chapter "Méthode différentielle conique", § "Validation", p.~78-80

Reflected and transmitted efficiencies of a lamellar grating under conical incidence, computed by the differential method and compared to the commercial RCWA-based software GSolver. Differences are of order 0.1%, considered satisfactory given that not all of GSolver's internal numerical parameters (in particular the precision of the profile definition) are known.
