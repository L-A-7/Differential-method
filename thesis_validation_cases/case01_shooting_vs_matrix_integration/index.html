Shooting method vs. matrix (Runge-Kutta) integration

Source: Table 2.1 (chap-1-2.tex, \label{tab:compare_shoot_matrix_formulation}), § "Algorithme d'intégration matriciel", chapter "Formulation stable et efficace des algorithmes et validation", p.~46-48

Internal cross-check: the new matrix (Runge-Kutta order 4) integration scheme proposed in the thesis to replace the classical shooting method is run on the same grating and compared order-by-order. This is not a comparison against another author's method, but a self-consistency test showing the two numerical schemes give identical reflected efficiencies (to the displayed precision) while the matrix scheme is dramatically faster: for N=100 and 10 integration steps, about 4500 s with the shooting method vs. ≈ 20 s with the matrix scheme, on the same Opteron 1.8 GHz processor.
