Pyramidal grating: convergence vs. N, reflected orders, vs. the Chandezon method

Source: Table 4.3 (chap-1-4.tex, \label{tab:md3D_validPyramid_r}) and Fig. 4.2 (\label{fig:md3D_convN_r_pyramid}), § "Tests et validation" > "Structures diélectriques", p.~97

Convergence of the reflected efficiencies r(0,-1) and r(0,0) as a function of the truncation order N, same pyramidal grating as case 08. The differential method (N=1..15) and the Chandezon method (N=3,5,7, as published) converge to compatible values, with the differential method converging faster.
