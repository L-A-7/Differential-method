Metallic (aluminium) sinusoidal grating vs. MFS and the Integral Method

Source: Table 2.4 (chap-1-2.tex, \label{tab:md1D_vs_MFS_IM_metal}), § "Validation" > "Comparaison avec d'autres méthodes numériques" > "Structures métalliques", p.~51

Reflected efficiencies from an aluminium sinusoidal grating, computed by the differential method (DM) and cross-checked against the Method of Fictitious Sources (MFS) and the Integral Method (IM, Maystre 1972). Agreement is good, though less tight than for the dielectric case above because a metallic grating needs more harmonics to reach convergence.
