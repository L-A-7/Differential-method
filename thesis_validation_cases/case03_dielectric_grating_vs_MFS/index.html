Dielectric sinusoidal grating vs. the Method of Fictitious Sources (MFS)

Source: Table 2.3 (chap-1-2.tex, \label{tab:md1D_MFS_dielec_r}), § "Validation" > "Comparaison avec d'autres méthodes numériques" > "Structures diélectriques", p.~50-51

Reflected efficiencies from a dielectric sinusoidal grating, computed by the differential method (with the S-matrix algorithm and, in TM, Li's fast Fourier factorization rules) and compared order-by-order to the Method of Fictitious Sources (MFS, Tayeb 1990/1994). Agreement is very good in both TE and TM (differences of order 1e-7 to 1e-6).
