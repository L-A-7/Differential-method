Reciprocity theorem check

Source: Table 2.2 (chap-1-2.tex, \label{tab:reciprocity}), § "Validation" > "Théorème de réciprocité", p.~49-50

Physical-law validation (not a comparison to another code): the electromagnetic reciprocity theorem states that swapping the source and the detector direction leaves the measured intensity unchanged. The grating is illuminated at θ_i = 5.00° and the efficiency is read at diffraction angle θ_n = 30.60°; the grating is then illuminated at θ_i = -30.60° and the efficiency is read at θ_n = -5.00°. Both configurations give the same efficiency (0.01144, highlighted below), confirming reciprocity.
