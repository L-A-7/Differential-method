Deep aluminium biperiodic grating: convergence vs. N

Source: Table 4.6 (chap-1-4.tex, \label{tab:md3D_conv_deepAlu}) and Fig. 4.4 (\label{fig:md3D_convAluNorm}), § "Tests et validation" > "Structures métalliques", p.~99-100

Convergence of the reflected efficiencies of a metallic (aluminium) biperiodic grating, 5x taller than the dielectric case (case 11), as a function of N. Convergence is satisfactory but slower than for the shallower dielectric grating: at N=5 the relative deviation from the N=15 value is about 5.5%. The companion figure (not reproduced as a table) shows that using the true, locally-defined normal vector is essential: fixing the normal vector arbitrarily along (Ox) needs roughly 10^6 times more computation time to reach the accuracy that the true-normal formulation reaches at N=1.
