Circular-hole array vs. Schuster, Moharam and Li's normal-vector RCWA formulations

Source: Table 4.7 (chap-1-4.tex, \label{tab:valid_schuster}) and Fig. 4.5 (\label{fig:valid_schuster}), § "Tests et validation" > "Structures invariantes selon (Oz)", p.~100-101

Convergence of the reflected order (0,0) for a (Oz)-invariant circular-hole array (so RCWA applies directly, no staircase approximation needed), as a function of N. Five formulations are compared: Moharam's classical RCWA (no special normal-vector treatment), Li's crossed-grating formulation, Schuster et al.'s two normal-vector fields (electrostatic and radial), and md3D using a radial normal-vector field identical to Schuster's. The md3D results and Schuster's radial-normal-vector results are in near-perfect agreement (data for the Moharam, Li and Schuster columns were provided by Schuster et al. and reproduced here with permission), while Moharam's classical formulation converges markedly more slowly — direct evidence that correctly defining the surface normal is what fixes RCWA/differential-method convergence in TM-like configurations.
