Convergence vs. N: differential method vs. RCWA (metallic grating, TE)

Source: Table 2.5 (chap-1-2.tex, \label{tab:rcwa_metall_TE}) and Fig. 2.?? (\label{fig:rcwa_metall_TM}), § "Cas de la méthode RCWA appliquée à des profils arbitraires", p.~58-60

Convergence of the reflected efficiency e(0) as a function of the truncation order N, for the differential method versus RCWA applied to a staircase (1000-step) approximation of the same sinusoidal metallic profile. In TE the two methods track each other closely (both reach the reference value from the MFS/IM tables, case 04, within ~1e-5). The companion TM comparison (not tabulated, only plotted in the thesis, Fig. \ref{fig:rcwa_metall_TM}) shows RCWA converging far more slowly in TM: at N=100 it is still off by about 30% from the reference, because a staircase profile's normal vector never matches the true continuous normal, which breaks the Fourier-factorization rules that make TM converge quickly.
