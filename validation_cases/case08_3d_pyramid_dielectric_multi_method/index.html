Dielectric biperiodic pyramidal grating vs. four independent methods

Source: Table 4.2 (chap-1-4.tex, \label{tab:valid_pyramid_dielec}), § "Tests et validation" > "Structures diélectriques", p.~96

Diffraction efficiencies of a biperiodic (crossed / 3D) dielectric pyramidal grating, computed by the 3D differential method (md3D, N=15) and compared to four independently published results: a curvilinear-coordinate-transformation method (Derrick et al. 1979), RCWA (Bräuer & Bryngdahl 1993), the Iterative Series Solution (Greffet et al. 1992) and the Chandezon method (Granet 1998). Good agreement is found with the Chandezon and ISS results; agreement is only approximate with the RCWA result (likely due to RCWA's poor convergence for this profile) and with the 1979 result (likely limited by the computing power available at the time).
