Differential method – PhD thesis validation test cases

Test cases extracted from L. Arnaud's PhD thesis (Institut Fresnel / Université Aix-Marseille III, defended July 2008), chapters “Formulation stable et efficace des algorithmes et validation”, “Méthode différentielle conique” and “Méthode différentielle tridimensionnelle”. These are the original numerical-validation cases used to establish the correctness of the differential-method implementation now hosted in md2D/ and md3D/ in this repository: each case reproduces a thesis table (and, where useful, the accompanying convergence figure) comparing the differential method against an independently published or commercial reference — the Method of Fictitious Sources, the Integral Method, RCWA, the Chandezon method, the Method of Variation of Boundaries, GSolver, or published results by Schuster et al. Every case folder has the raw values in data.csv and a rendered index.html with the parameters, an SVG plot where relevant, and the full data table.
