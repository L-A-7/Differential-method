Abstract
:(250
words
max)
One
of
the
most
precise
and
effective
characterization
technique
used
nowadays
in
the
semiconductors
industry
is
called
scatterometry.
It
consists
of
solving
the
electromagnetic
inverse
problem
after
appropriate
ellipsometric
measurements
of
a
periodic
test
structure.
The
roughness
present
on
the
grating
test
structure
can
be
classified
in
several
categories
(Line
Edge
Roughness,
Line
Width
Roughness,
etc...)
and
can
impact
the
electronic
response
of
the
device.
With
the
constant
reduction
of
the
critical
dimensions,
the
roughness
may
become
of
an
encreasing
importance
relatively
to
the
average
line
width,
and
thus
more
important
to
characterize.
We
have
developped
an
electromagnetic
model
able
to
take
into
acount
both
the
scattering
by
the
periodic
part
of
the
structure
and
by
the
roughness.
Our
simulations
confirm
that
the
classical
scatterometry,
measuring
only
the
specular
beam,
is
almost
unsensible
to
roughness.
We
show
how
the
roughness
present
on
the
grating
impacts
the
optical
signatures
and
how
it
is
possible
to
characterize
it
through
appropriate
measurements
and
calculations.
